Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · SocietyOS is a brand of The Nyx & Raven Ranch, LLC

1. What We Collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, and password (stored only as a salted cryptographic hash — we can never read it).
  • Membership data: your tier, hardware selections, and device choices.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. SocietyOS never stores card numbers. We see only your payment status.
  • Ambassador data: referral codes, referral counts, and payout preferences.

2. What We Never Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not read your passwords, wallet keys, or Tangem credentials.

3. What We Share — Only to Operate Your Membership

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • FreeCast — streaming activation for Society TV (you create that account directly).
  • Umbrel / Tangem — hardware activation and warranty support.
  • Each third party processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Sovereignty by Design

Your SocietyOS files, media, and assistant memory on Umbrel-based Nodes live on your hardware, in your home — not on our servers. SocietyOS cannot read what is stored on your own Node.

5. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of your data at any time.
  • Request correction or deletion of your account data.
  • Cancel renewal and close your account by contacting hello@societyos.world.

6. Cookies

SocietyOS uses only functional session storage required for sign-in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Security

Passwords use PBKDF2 salting and hashing (100,000 iterations). Access to member records is restricted to authorized SocietyOS administration. Rewards and payout data are held in a private ledger and never shared with payment processors beyond what is needed to pay you.

8. Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
