Core Capabilities

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

SocietyOS is not just a browser. It's a complete digital environment with AI at its core, designed for those who demand control, privacy, and power.

🧠

Agentic AI Core

A built-in AI assistant that navigates, creates, codes, researches, and manages your workflow. Select your favorite LLM models — you're always in control. Calendar, mail, messaging, and more, unified in one intelligent interface.

🛡️

Absolute Data Sovereignty

Cloud-based convenience or fully sovereign local processing. Your data, your rules. No third-party processing, no hidden tracking, no compromises. Gold Business Ambassador tier includes dedicated infrastructure.

📺

Society TV

A custom media player that rivals the competition, integrated directly into your browser. Curated entertainment, live concerts, blockbuster premieres, and exclusive content — all part of The Nyx & Raven Ranch ecosystem.

🔌

Extension Services

First-party and collaborator extensions designed specifically for SocietyOS. Connect your services, integrate your tools, and build on a platform engineered for deep, meaningful integration — not just add-ons.

⚡

Dual-Engine Performance

WebKit on Apple platforms for native performance. Chromium Embedded Framework on Android and custom systems. The right engine for every platform, with a unified AI layer abstracting the differences away.

🔗

NODES on Solana

Blockchain-integrated membership through NODES on Solana. Tokenized access, governance rights, and a financial ecosystem that makes your membership an asset, not just a subscription.
